The unmodified geometry is used in an isolated visual experiment. DocClin's existing
illustrative textures are projected onto it with custom shaders. This is not a
validated anatomical mesh, a scan of the depicted woman/man, or a clinical tool.
Both gender presentations currently share this study geometry.
